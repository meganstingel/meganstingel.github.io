Me, Myself and I

I've always straddled the fence between numbers and steps, and intuition and beauty. Am I better at one than the other? Depends on the day. Today I am teaching CIT and Digital Media Design. And my students on both sides are better for it. My design students learn the importance of understanding the code behind software like Dreamweaver, and communicating effectively through visual hierarchy. My IT students learn that spitting out code is only half useful; if there is no design behind your code, how can anyone use it? I call it form taught by function, and fuction taught by form.
